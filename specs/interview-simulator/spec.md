# Interview Simulator Feature Spec

## Summary
Build a browser-based interview simulator with AI-powered question answering, resume upload support, transcript export, and a polished UI.

## Goals
- Provide an interactive interview experience.
- Support PDF, DOCX, and TXT resume upload.
- Save and export interview transcripts cleanly.
- Include accessible theme controls and dark mode.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Users can start an interview and receive AI responses.
- Resume uploads are parsed and used by the interview engine.
- Transcript export generates a downloadable file and cleans up temporary state.
- The UI offers both light and dark modes.
